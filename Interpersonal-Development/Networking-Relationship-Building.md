# Networking & Relationship Building

## Overview

Building and maintaining professional and personal relationships for career and personal growth.

## Networking Strategies

### Building Your Network
- Attend events
- Join communities
- Online networking
- Professional associations
- Alumni networks

### Networking Mindset
- Give before you ask
- Genuine interest in others
- Long-term perspective
- Mutual benefit
- Authentic connections

### Networking Events
- Preparation
- Elevator pitch
- Conversation starters
- Active listening
- Follow-up

## Relationship Maintenance

### Regular Contact
- Check in periodically
- Share relevant information
- Offer help
- Celebrate their successes
- Stay connected

### Value Exchange
- Provide value
- Share resources
- Make introductions
- Offer expertise
- Support their goals

## Contact Management

### Contact Organization
- CRM systems
- Contact details
- Relationship notes
- Interaction history
- Follow-up reminders

### Relationship Tracking
- Last contact date
- Important dates
- Shared interests
- Mutual connections
- Relationship strength

## Follow-Up Systems

### After Meetings
- Send thank you
- Share resources
- Follow through on promises
- Schedule next contact

### Regular Touchpoints
- Quarterly check-ins
- Birthday greetings
- Career milestones
- Shared interests
- Value-add opportunities

## Online Presence

### LinkedIn
- Complete profile
- Regular updates
- Engage with content
- Share valuable content
- Build thought leadership

### Other Platforms
- Twitter/X
- Industry forums
- Professional communities
- Personal website
- Content creation
