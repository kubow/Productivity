# Conflict Resolution

## Overview

Effective strategies for resolving conflicts and having difficult conversations.

## Conflict Resolution Frameworks

### Collaborative Problem-Solving
- Focus on interests, not positions
- Find win-win solutions
- Separate people from problem
- Use objective criteria

### The 5 Steps
1. **Identify the problem**: What's the real issue?
2. **Understand perspectives**: Listen to all sides
3. **Generate solutions**: Brainstorm options
4. **Evaluate options**: Consider pros and cons
5. **Agree on solution**: Find common ground

## Difficult Conversations

### Preparation
- Clarify your goal
- Understand their perspective
- Choose the right time and place
- Prepare what to say

### During the Conversation
- Stay calm
- Listen actively
- Use "I" statements
- Avoid blame
- Focus on behavior, not character

### After the Conversation
- Follow up
- Check understanding
- Monitor progress
- Adjust as needed

## Communication Techniques

### Active Listening
- Give full attention
- Reflect back what you hear
- Ask clarifying questions
- Show empathy
- Avoid interrupting

### Nonviolent Communication
- Observations (not evaluations)
- Feelings
- Needs
- Requests

### De-escalation
- Stay calm
- Acknowledge emotions
- Find common ground
- Focus on solutions
- Take breaks if needed

## Common Scenarios

- Workplace conflicts
- Team disagreements
- Customer complaints
- Personal relationships
- Family conflicts

## Prevention

- Clear communication
- Set expectations
- Address issues early
- Build relationships
- Regular check-ins
