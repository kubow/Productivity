# Exercise and Movement

## Overview

Regular movement supports physical and mental well-being and can improve energy and work capacity. The most useful routine is safe, repeatable, and appropriate to the person’s current health and ability.

## A Balanced Pattern

- **Everyday movement:** Walk, use stairs, perform household tasks, and break up long periods of sitting.
- **Aerobic activity:** Choose an activity that raises breathing and heart rate at an appropriate intensity.
- **Strength:** Train major movement patterns with suitable resistance and recovery.
- **Mobility and balance:** Practice ranges of motion and balance relevant to daily life.
- **Recovery:** Increase volume gradually and allow adaptation.

## During Desk-Based Work

- Change position regularly; no single posture needs to be held all day.
- Use brief movement breaks between focus blocks or meetings.
- Adjust the workspace so common movements are comfortable.
- Build activity into transitions rather than depending on motivation after work.

## Build the Habit

- Start with a dose you can repeat.
- Choose activities you tolerate or enjoy.
- Schedule the time and prepare equipment in advance.
- Track consistency and gradual progress, not only intense sessions.
- Reduce the plan during difficult weeks instead of abandoning it.

Stop and seek appropriate medical guidance for alarming symptoms, significant injury, or uncertainty about exercise with an existing condition.
