# People and Ideas

This is an index of thinkers, authors, and practitioners whose ideas appear across the knowledge base. It explains why each person is relevant and points to the topic where the idea is applied. Inclusion is attribution and orientation, not an endorsement of every claim or work by that person.

The [Resource Library](./Resource-Library.md) separately indexes files stored in this repository.

## Thinking and Learning

### Richard Feynman

Physicist and teacher associated with explaining a subject in simple language to expose gaps in understanding. See [Learning and Knowledge Management](./Personal-Development/Learning-Knowledge-Management.md) and the discussion of self-deception in [Cognitive Biases](./Personal-Development/Cognitive-Biases.md).

### Charlie Munger

Investor and essayist known for advocating a broad “latticework” of mental models, inversion, and multidisciplinary judgment. See [Decision-Making Frameworks](./Personal-Development/Decision-Making-Frameworks.md).

### Constance Missimer

Philosopher and educator focused on critical thinking, including its application in professional settings. See [Critical Thinking](./Personal-Development/Critical-Thinking.md) and [Critical Thinking at Work](https://www.criticalthinkingatwork.com/).

### Edward de Bono

Author associated with lateral thinking and *Six Thinking Hats*. See [Creative Thinking](./Personal-Development/Creative-Thinking.md) and [Decision-Making Frameworks](./Personal-Development/Decision-Making-Frameworks.md).

### Daniel Kahneman and Amos Tversky

Researchers whose work on heuristics, biases, and judgment under uncertainty shaped modern behavioral decision research. See [Cognitive Biases and Distortions](./Personal-Development/Cognitive-Biases.md).

### Carl Sagan

Scientist and communicator known for connecting scientific skepticism, evidence, and public reasoning. See [Critical Thinking](./Personal-Development/Critical-Thinking.md).

## Productivity and Focus

### David Allen

Creator of the Getting Things Done methodology: capture, clarify, organize, reflect, and engage. See [Task Management](./Productivity-Techniques/Task-Management.md).

### Cal Newport

Author associated with deep work, attention protection, shutdown rituals, and intentional technology use. See [Deep Work and Focus](./Productivity-Techniques/Deep-Work-Focus.md) and [Digital Minimalism](./Productivity-Techniques/Digital-Minimalism.md).

### Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi

Psychologist associated with research on flow: deep absorption in appropriately challenging activity. See [Deep Work and Focus](./Productivity-Techniques/Deep-Work-Focus.md) and [Motivation Drivers](./Self-Management/motivation/Motivation-Drivers.md).

### Brian Tracy and Greg McKeown

Authors associated respectively with acting on important work and deliberately eliminating lower-value commitments. See [Prioritization](./Productivity-Techniques/Prioritization.md) and [Goal Setting](./Self-Management/Goal-Setting.md).

## Self-Management and Well-being

### Stephen Covey

Author associated with proactivity, principle-centered behavior, and prioritizing important work. See [Proactivity](./Self-Management/proactive/Proactivity.md) and [Goal Setting](./Self-Management/Goal-Setting.md).

### Carol Dweck

Psychologist known for research and writing on fixed and growth mindsets. See [Mindset, Resilience, and Positivity](./Self-Management/Mindset.md).

### Charles Duhigg and James Clear

Authors who popularized practical models of habit cues, routines, rewards, environment design, and identity-based habits. See [Habit Systems](./Self-Management/Habit-Systems.md).

### Edward Deci and Richard Ryan

Psychologists who developed Self-Determination Theory, emphasizing autonomy, competence, and relatedness. See [Motivation Drivers](./Self-Management/motivation/Motivation-Drivers.md).

### Jim Loehr and Tony Schwartz

Authors associated with managing energy and recovery alongside time. See [Energy Management](./Self-Management/Energy-Management.md) and [Recovery and Deliberate Rest](./Self-Management/Recovery-Rest.md).

### Jon Kabat-Zinn

Teacher and author associated with mindfulness-based stress reduction. See [Meditation and Mindfulness](./Self-Management/Meditate.md) and [Stress Management](./Self-Management/Stress-Management-Techniques.md).

## Communication and Relationships

### Dale Carnegie

Author associated with interpersonal attention, rapport, and constructive influence. See [Effective Listening](./Interpersonal-Development/Effective-Listening.md) and [Networking and Relationship Building](./Interpersonal-Development/Networking-Relationship-Building.md).

### Keith Ferrazzi

Author associated with building and maintaining professional relationships through generosity and regular contact. See [Networking and Relationship Building](./Interpersonal-Development/Networking-Relationship-Building.md).

### Roger Fisher and William Ury

Authors associated with interest-based negotiation and alternatives to negotiated agreement. See [Negotiation](./Interpersonal-Development/Negotiation.md) and [Conflict Resolution](./Interpersonal-Development/Conflict-Resolution.md).

### Marshall Rosenberg

Developer of Nonviolent Communication, emphasizing observations, feelings, needs, and requests. See [Conflict Resolution](./Interpersonal-Development/Conflict-Resolution.md).

### Kim Scott

Author associated with combining direct challenge and personal care in feedback. See [Giving and Receiving Feedback](./Interpersonal-Development/Feedback.md).

## Career and Leadership

### Richard Bolles

Author known for practical career exploration and job-search guidance. See [Career Planning and Job Search](./Career-Development/Career-Planning-Job-Search.md).

### John Maxwell

Author associated with accessible models of leadership development. See [Leadership Models](./Career-Development/Leadership-Models.md).

### Simon Sinek

Author associated with articulating purpose and using “why” to frame leadership communication. See [Leadership Models](./Career-Development/Leadership-Models.md) and [Storytelling](./Interpersonal-Development/Storytelling.md).
