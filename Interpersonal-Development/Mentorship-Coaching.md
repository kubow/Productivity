# Mentorship & Coaching

## Overview

Mentorship is sustained guidance from someone with more experience; coaching is often more structured and question-led to unlock the other person’s own solutions. Both develop people and strengthen teams—and support your own growth when you are the mentee or coachee.

## Why It Matters for Productivity

- **Faster learning** — Avoid repeating others’ mistakes; get feedback and direction.
- **Better decisions** — Mentors and coaches help you see blind spots; see [Feedback](./Feedback.md) and [Cognitive Biases](../Personal-Development/Cognitive-Biases.md).
- **Career and performance** — Career advancement and job performance often depend on guidance and sponsorship.
- **Team output** — Developing others multiplies impact; see [Leadership Models](../Career-Development/Leadership-Models.md) and [Delegation](../Career-Development/Delegation.md).

## As a Mentee: Getting a Mentor

- **Clarify what you need** — Skills, career direction, industry insight, or sponsorship.
- **Look in and outside your org** — Manager, senior colleagues, alumni, industry groups; see [Networking and Relationship Building](./Networking-Relationship-Building.md).
- **Ask clearly** — “I’d value your advice on X. Would you have 20 minutes every few weeks to chat?”
- **Prepare** — Bring specific questions or situations; respect their time; follow up.
- **Give back** — Share progress, thank them, offer help when you can.

## As a Mentor: Giving Guidance

- **Listen first** — Understand their situation and goals; see [Effective Listening](./Effective-Listening.md).
- **Share experience, not mandates** — “Here’s what I did in a similar situation” rather than “You must do X.”
- **Ask questions** — Help them think it through; don’t always solve it for them.
- **Set expectations** — How often you’ll meet, how they can prepare, what’s in and out of scope.
- **Respect boundaries** — Your time and expertise; see [Boundaries](./Boundaries.md) and [Saying No](./Saying-No.md).

## Coaching Basics (When You Coach Others)

- **Ask more, tell less** — “What have you tried?” “What would success look like?” “What’s one step you could take?”
- **Hold the focus** — Keep the conversation on their goal, not your advice.
- **Accountability** — Agree on one small next step and when they’ll do it.
- **Neutrality** — Stay curious; avoid imposing your solution.

## Receiving Coaching or Mentoring

- **Be open** — To feedback and different perspectives; see [Feedback](./Feedback.md) and [Mindset](../Self-Management/Mindset.md).
- **Do the work** — Try what you discuss; report back.
- **Reflect** — Use [Review & Reflection Systems](../Self-Management/Review-Reflection-Systems.md) to track what you’re learning and applying.
