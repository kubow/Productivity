# Meditation and Mindfulness

## Overview

Mindfulness is the practice of noticing present experience with less automatic judgment. Meditation is one way to train it. The goal is not to eliminate thoughts, but to notice attention wandering and return intentionally.

## A Simple Practice

1. Choose a quiet-enough place and a short period, such as two to ten minutes.
2. Sit, stand, or walk in a stable and comfortable way.
3. Place attention on breathing, bodily sensation, sound, or movement.
4. When attention wanders, acknowledge it gently.
5. Return to the chosen anchor.
6. At the end, notice your state before continuing the day.

## Everyday Mindfulness

- Take one deliberate breath before replying in a tense conversation.
- Give full attention to one routine activity such as walking or washing dishes.
- Label a strong feeling before acting on it.
- Use a short body scan during a transition or break.
- Notice thoughts as mental events rather than immediate instructions.

## Building the Habit

- Start smaller than your ambition suggests.
- Attach practice to an existing routine.
- Track showing up, not whether the session felt calm.
- Try guided practice if silence is unhelpful.
- Stop or seek qualified support if practice repeatedly causes significant distress.
