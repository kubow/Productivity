# Nutrition and Hydration

## Overview

Regular nourishment and hydration support health, energy, and concentration. Individual needs vary with health, medication, body size, activity, climate, culture, and access; this note offers general habits rather than a prescriptive diet.

## Practical Foundations

- Eat a varied pattern centered on minimally processed foods when practical.
- Include vegetables and fruit, sources of protein, fiber-rich carbohydrates, and appropriate fats.
- Keep convenient nourishing options available for demanding days.
- Notice whether long gaps between meals affect energy, mood, or concentration.
- Limit foods and drinks that predictably disrupt your energy, digestion, or sleep rather than applying an all-or-nothing rule.

## Hydration

- Drink regularly and respond to thirst.
- Increase attention to fluids during heat, illness, or prolonged physical activity.
- Remember that fluid needs vary; a fixed volume is not appropriate for everyone.
- Seek medical guidance when a health condition or medication affects fluid or electrolyte needs.

## Make the Habit Easy

- Plan a small set of repeatable meals rather than relying on daily decisions.
- Prepare or portion food when energy is higher.
- Keep water visible and accessible.
- Use shopping lists and staple ingredients.
- Treat missed plans as information, then simplify the system.
